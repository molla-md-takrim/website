## Brand & Style
The design system establishes an unapologetic, high-velocity presence tailored for enterprise decision-makers and founders scaling through AI automation. Rooted in conversion-engineered direct-response principles, the interface strips away decorative fluff in favor of hyper-legible metrics, authoritative typography, and intense call-to-action focus.

The visual style unites high-contrast editorial weight with modern SaaS precision. Stark black typography over soft off-white canvas sets an institutional, value-dense foundation, punctuated by electric violet accents that guide the eye directly toward conversion funnels, ROI calculators, and consultation bookings.

## Colors
The palette leverages stark contrast ratios exceeding WCAG AAA standards for critical actions and body text. 

- **Primary CTA Accent (`#6F00FF`)**: Electric Purple, reserved exclusively for forward progress, primary triggers, active toggles, and conversion hotspots. Hover states shift to `#5900CC` with an elevated glow.
- **Secondary Accent (`#806BB8`)**: Muted Amethyst, applied to high-tier announcement pills, eyebrow tags, secondary badges, and category labels.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#10B981`)**: High-growth Emerald, deployed solely for quantitative proof metrics, revenue lift badges, and uptime indicators.
- **Deep Ink Neutral (`#171827`)**: The dominant anchor for all primary headings, bold microcopy, and solid secondary buttons.
- **Surfaces**: Canvas is grounded in soft warm off-white (`#F8F8F4`), contrasting against pure white (`#FFFFFF`) layered cards and structured `#F0F0F0` section backgrounds.
- **Borders**: Hairline structural borders use a muted slate-gray (`#E4E4E7`).

## Typography
Typographic scale enforces clear information hierarchy through tension: brutal, punchy geometric sans headlines set in all-caps Montserrat juxtaposed against hyper-rational, fluid Inter body text.

- **Headlines & Metric Figures**: Montserrat set at 800 or 900 weight. Hero and Section titles frequently utilize all-caps tracking (`letter-spacing: -0.02em` to `0.05em`) for commanding weight.
- **Body & Data Metrics**: Inter handles all multi-line copy, descriptions, data tables, and interactive states to optimize scanning speed and prevent cognitive fatigue.
- **Eyebrows / Overlines**: Micro-caps (`label-caps`) styled with `letter-spacing: 0.1em` in uppercase, setting context for every major card and value proposition.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a fixed-max-width 12-column structural grid system capped at `1280px` for optimal reading scan lines.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 32px gutters, 80px side margins. High-density cards sit in 3 or 4-column groupings.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 24px gutters, 48px side margins. Cards adapt into 2-column matrices.
- **Mobile (< 767px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, 24px side margins. All multi-column blocks stack into single-column vertical flows.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Large section blocks maintain `space-2xl` to `space-3xl` gaps, framing each conversion step as an isolated psychological decision point.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through flat tonal planes, crisp structural borders, and purposeful hard-edge shadows rather than soft atmospheric blurs.

- **Surface Levels**:
  - `Base`: `#F8F8F4` Canvas.
  - `Raised (Level 1)`: `#FFFFFF` Cards framed with `1px solid #E4E4E7`.
  - `Interactive Hover (Level 2)`: Elevates with `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(23, 24, 39, 0.08)` and border shifts to `#171827`.
  - `Active Focus / Primary Pop (Level 3)`: Primary CTAs utilize an energetic purple drop glow: `0 8px 20px -2px rgba(111, 0, 255, 0.35)`.
- **Dividers & Outlines**: Clean 1px edges delimit dense data, avoiding heavy shadow pollution to maintain executive clarity.

## Shapes
Shapes reinforce an aggressive, high-execution agency identity. 

- **Primary CTAs & Form Fields**: Strict 6px to 8px border radius (`0.375rem` - `0.5rem`), eliminating bubbly shapes to convey technical rigor and precision.
- **Cards & Tier Modules**: 8px (`0.5rem`) corner rounding maintains monolithic solidity.
- **Badges & Pills**: Full-bleed pill radius (`9999px`) reserved only for micro announcement banners, status indicators, and client logos to soften internal card contrast.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#6F00FF`, Text `#FFFFFF`, font Montserrat 800 uppercase (`label-caps`), padding `16px 32px`, border radius `6px`. Hover: Background `#5900CC`, transform `translateY(-2px)`, shadow `0 8px 20px -2px rgba(111, 0, 255, 0.4)`. Active: `translateY(0)`.
- **Secondary Action**: Background `#171827`, Text `#FFFFFF`, border radius `6px`, padding `16px 28px`. Hover: Background `#26283E`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent background, text `#171827`, border `1.5px solid #171827`, radius `6px`. Hover: Inverted colors.

### Cards & Tiers
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E4E4E7` borders and 8px radius.
- **Featured Tier Card**: Border switches to `2px solid #6F00FF`, badge anchored at the top right, subtle background gradient overlay at top edge (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(111, 0, 255, 0.03) 0%, transparent 100%)`).

### Chips & Social Proof Badges
- **Announcement Pill**: Background `rgba(128, 107, 184, 0.12)`, text `#806BB8`, border `1px solid rgba(128, 107, 184, 0.3)`, pill-radius.
- **Metric Highlight Pill**: Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`, text `#047857`, label font Montserrat 800.

### Input Fields
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #D4D4D8`, padding `14px 16px`, typography Inter 16px.
- Focus: Border `1.5px solid #6F00FF`, outline `3px solid rgba(111, 0, 255, 0.15)`.

### Lists & Feature Vectors

- Quantitative lead text rendered in bold Inter (`fontWeight: 700`) followed by muted supporting copy (`#52525B`).
